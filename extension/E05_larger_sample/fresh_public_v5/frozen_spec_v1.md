# E5 larger-sample comparison: frozen scientific specification v1

Status: [ASSUMPTION, prospective rules] Authorized E5 design freeze. This is a review-informed follow-up, not a blinded preregistration. No E5 fit, eligibility count, interval or maturity result has been read or computed before this freeze. Earlier S02a, E2, E3 and E4 outcomes and source limitations were already known; a complete independent history of earlier outcome access cannot be established from these files. The SHA-256 manifest records the exact UTF-8 bytes of this version. A changed scientific rule requires a new version and hash before the affected outcome is read. Freeze executable code, environment, input snapshots, file-selection manifest and seeds before any E5 outcome computation.

## Source hierarchy and scope

[VERIFIED protocol] The binding design is `extension/E05_larger_sample.md`, with S01 `analysis_protocol.md`, `protocol_core.py`, S02a `spec_v1.md` and accepted E1 v2 protocol providing inherited details. E4's `event_inventory_spec.md` supplies the causal onset detector. The E4 catalog is an intake inventory, not an E5 eligible cohort. E5 only; no release-file edits or later-stage execution.

[ASSUMPTION, input gate] Candidate series are the historical four-maturity VIX term structure (VIX9D, VIX30D, VIX3M, VIX6M) and the E4 official Cboe VXN, RVX, OVX, GVZ histories, each subject to complete snapshot, source rights and comparability checks. VSTOXX, MOVE and OptionMetrics enter only if official/entitled observations, definitions and usage rights are established before a new frozen input-selection version; their absence is recorded, never filled with a proxy. The existing proposed WRDS SPX 30-day/50-delta call panel is not an observed or comparable added cohort. Source/index identity, option horizon, underlying and methodology are reported separately. Cross-market pooling is secondary and descriptive; no numerical transport of VIX-only E2 N80 is assumed.

## Onset, anchor, panel and cohort

[VERIFIED inherited] On each source's own valid observed-session calendar, candidate onset at t if log(V_t/V_(t-1)) >= log(1+7/20) or log(V_t/V_(t-5)) >= log(1+10/20); previous observations needed by the selected test must be valid. Accept only if >30 calendar days after the last accepted onset. No named-crisis merge or later peak enters onset detection. For the legacy VIX term-structure cohort, retain the inherited S02a merged catalog and explicitly identify that it differs from the new causal E4 catalog; do not silently pool events with different definitions. If a common causal catalog is needed for a cross-index summary, construct it under this rule before fitting and report legacy results separately.

[VERIFIED inherited] A candidate panel requires 250 strictly pre-onset valid closes for its own series; baseline mu is their mean log close. Peak anchor is the first argmax of observed closes from onset through +10 source sessions inclusive. This is retrospective descriptive anchoring, never a forecast origin. Require complete observed dates from anchor through anchor+120 inclusive (121 levels, fitted times t=s..120), positive finite closes, and denominator log(V_anchor)-mu >1e-8. Set z(t)=(log(V_(anchor+t))-mu)/(log(V_anchor)-mu). Keep negative undershoots. Do not impute, floor, extrapolate or reset elapsed t at a fit start. Exclude a panel if the next accepted onset in the same source occurs on or before its final required response date; retain exact reason. The E4 onset-through-119 screen alone is insufficient after a later anchor. A scalar source is one series, not a term structure.

[ASSUMPTION, explicit construction] Build a full eligible-event panel independently per index. For genuine same-underlying term structures, separately build a common-event panel requiring the same onset ID and complete valid panel at every maturity being compared; preserve an event's paired maturity observations. Do not pair coincident dates across different underlyings. Report all screened IDs and exclusions, full and common cohort counts and data-use date ranges. Equal weight to each event trajectory within an index: y_m(t)=mean_i z_im(t). The primary per-index cohort is full; paired maturity contrasts use the common-event panel and cannot be inferred from unpaired full panels. A pooled cross-index display gives each included index equal weight after each index's event mean, and remains secondary because the markets and index methods differ.

## Fits and historical constants

[VERIFIED inherited definitions] All families have free b in [-2,2] and amplitude a in [0,5]. E1: b+a exp(-t/tau), tau in [0.2,5000]. E2: b+a[w exp(-t/tau1)+(1-w)exp(-t/tau2)], w in [0,1], tau1<=tau2, both in [0.2,5000]. PL: b+a(1+t/c)^(-p), c in [0.001,100000], p in [0.01,1000]. SE: b+a exp(-(t/tau)^beta), tau in [0.2,5000], beta in [0.1,1]. Units of every tau/c are observed trading sessions. The E1 relaxation constant is the fitted tau parameter, not log(2)*tau, a winner-selected parameter, or an exponential equivalent inferred from PL/SE.

[VERIFIED inherited optimizer] For each family and fit start s in {1,5}, use points t=s..120 without resetting t; primary start 5, secondary start 1 as prescribed by E5 (overrides S01's historical start-1 primary). Fit the arithmetic event-mean response with 32 seed-keyed bounded trust-region least-squares starts, SciPy TRF, ftol=xtol=gtol=1e-8, max_nfev=5000. Use `protocol_core.BOUNDS`, `curve`, `ordered`, `fit` and `score` semantics; deterministic base seed 5902026 with keys `(Extension,E5,index,cohort,start,family,draw_id)` and a separate key for each cluster resample. Successful fit requires one finite converged start; save every start, failed status, active/boundary mask, SSE, parameters and point score n*log(SSE/n)+2k (SSE zero => -infinity), with k=3/5/4/4 and n=121-s. Rank scores descriptively and report all four, including failures; no point-score significance test. Do not drop a hard index or failed draw silently.

[VERIFIED historical context, not an E5 estimate] The S02a full-event, free-baseline, 120-session, start-5 E1 point tau values in `stages/S02a_pooled/pooled_summary.json` are VIX9D 19.28549701920492, VIX30D 27.779238995251557, VIX3M 36.13774363372049, VIX6M 40.582271389181905 sessions. Their exact panel definition and source are stated to avoid relabeling them as E5 results. E5 will estimate fresh per-index taus and 95% cluster intervals when estimable.

## Full-footprint overlap clusters and resampling

[ASSUMPTION, specified implementation of E5 brief] Each included event's data-use footprint is the closed calendar-date interval from the first of its 250 pre-onset observations through anchor+120. Connect two event/security footprints if their calendar-date intervals intersect, across all included sources, indices and maturities; take transitive components. Shared event IDs across maturity panels remain in the same component. Rebuild components after final eligibility, not from E4's onset-through-119 clusters. Save memberships, first/last dates, indices, event and security IDs, overlap edges, and effective component count K for each full/common test set. Calendar-connected components are the conservative resampling units; their statistical independence is an assumption to assess, not established by disjoint dates alone.

[ASSUMPTION, deterministic bootstrap] Draw 999 vectors of K cluster IDs with replacement using `protocol_core.seed('Extension','E5','cluster_bootstrap',panel_key,draw_id)` and NumPy's recorded RNG version. Include all event trajectories, all maturities and all securities belonging to a sampled component; duplicate an entire component when drawn repeatedly. For each draw, recompute equal-event within-index means and refit every family at each start. Preserve pairing for maturity contrasts by using the same draw vector and common-event IDs. Save sampled cluster IDs, multiplicities, fit records and failures per draw. A draw lacking a required index/common event or a valid family fit is failed for the affected estimand; report failed and valid draw counts, never replace it with an unlogged redraw. Require >=10 distinct observed base clusters and >=900 valid of 999 draws for an inferential interval or contrast; otherwise label it descriptive/not estimable. The ten-cluster rule is prescribed; the 900-draw operational completion threshold is fixed here to avoid opportunistic filtering. Use linear-interpolated 2.5th/97.5th percentiles on valid draws for 95% intervals, and a two-sided sign test p=2*min((1+#draws<=0)/(B+1),(1+#draws>=0)/(B+1)), clipped at 1, for a paired tau difference; report bootstrap resolution and any zero differences. If this sign convention fails under ties or too few valid draws, report unestimable rather than changing it after outcomes.

## Maturity contrasts and multiplicity

[ASSUMPTION, fixed comparison family] Only true comparable term structures permit maturity inference. On a four-maturity VIX common-event panel, eligible contrasts are E1 tau(VIX9D)-tau(VIX30D), tau(VIX3M)-tau(VIX30D), and tau(VIX6M)-tau(VIX30D), each at primary start 5. The same three contrasts at start 1 are sensitivity descriptions, with paired intervals but not extra confirmatory tests. If a documented comparable additional maturity panel exists under a separately frozen input version, append its reference and comparison set before fitting. Scalar VXN/RVX/OVX/GVZ and a lone standardized WRDS 30-day series have no maturity contrast. For each eligible primary contrast, refit on the common-event panel in every whole-cluster draw, report point difference, paired 95% percentile interval and raw two-sided bootstrap p. Apply Holm step-down adjustment once across the predeclared eligible primary contrasts, including every estimable contrast; list unestimable slots and reasons. No inference from overlap of marginal tau intervals, no post-hoc selected contrasts, and no likelihood-based point-score p-values.

## Execution, evidence and interpretation

[VERIFIED binding rule] Before reading E5 outcomes, save exact source snapshots/hashes, immutable input selection and code hashes, environment, seeds and this spec's SHA-256. Record known E2/E3/E4 selection and historical access limits. Checkpoint atomically by fit panel and bootstrap draw with unit ID, hashes, seed, elapsed time, completed/failed/remaining units. An execution run lasts at most eight hours, with scheduling stopped at seven hours, worker watchdog/termination by 7h45 and export before eight hours. On continuation reconcile workers, verify all hashes, reuse only valid complete units and rerun missing/corrupt units under original seeds; never pool scientific versions.

[VERIFIED acceptance boundary] Report source coverage, rights, cohort/exclusion/cluster tables, four-family scores and diagnostics, E1 taus, paired maturity contrasts, raw/Holm p values, number ledger and full draw records. Vera independently verifies every reported number and membership before stage acceptance. Fewer than ten full-footprint clusters means descriptive results, not 999 independent observations. Missing source/data/review is PARTIAL; invalid clustering, baseline leakage or hidden selection is FAIL. No power-law claim follows from a descriptive score alone.
