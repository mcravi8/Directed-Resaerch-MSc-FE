# S02b calibration execution freeze, version 1

This addendum is frozen before generated v2 calibration outcomes. It implements D-52 without changing its gates. Empirical log VIX30D uses all 9,234 observed closes through August 31, 2026; population SD and centered Pearson autocorrelation at lags 1, 22, 63. The empirical raw comparator is `protocol_core.detect` on these observed closes, with no named-event merge. Simulations use the same detector, so catalog-specific Lehman/GFC merging has no counterpart. The first maximum in the inclusive onset..onset+10 window is the argmax anchor. Generated dates are the exact 9,234 empirical observed dates, preserving calendar cooldown and observed-session offsets.

Calibration fits theoretical stationary covariance moments. Mean and SD equal the observed values by construction. Optimize the process ACF parameters with bounded deterministic multi-start least squares, minimizing sum of squared ACF residuals divided by tolerances [0.015, 0.07, 0.10] at lags [1,22,63]. OU tau is in [1,2000] sessions; two OU taus are ordered in [1,2000], fast variance fraction [0.01,0.99]; ARFIMA(0,d,0) has d in [0.01,0.499]. A theoretical fit passes only if every absolute ACF residual is at most its tolerance. The strict componentwise rule prevents a good weighted total from masking a bad lag. These tolerances are fixed practical moment tolerances, not confidence intervals. There is no recovery-result tuning or second fit after seeing generated paths.

Each process has 11 independent 9,234-session stationary paths, seeds keyed by `core.seed('S02b','calibration',process,index)`. OU components start at their invariant Gaussian distribution. ARFIMA uses exact stationary covariance via checked circulant embedding. The middle ordered statistic (sixth of 11) is reported for every diagnostic separately. Generated-path median moment residuals must each meet the same componentwise tolerances; mean and SD tolerances are 0.03 log points, applied to the median. The median raw detected-event count must lie in [20,50], and median paired-anchor common eligible event count must be >=12. These are distinct medians; all 11 path counts remain in the diagnostic table. Failure of any gate means NOT CALIBRATABLE under this frozen method. Positive denominator >1e-8, 250 valid strictly pre-onset observations, complete 120-session horizon before the next onset, and both anchors are required for common eligibility. No truncation, imputation or event-count adjustment.

The calibration fit starts are fixed in code, with the deterministic order recorded there. For 2OU, the optimizer uses log time constants and the fast variance fraction. The same constraints apply to generation. A Monte Carlo median is a calibration diagnostic and does not estimate recovery.

The inherited collapse test for a winning E2 is `w<0.05 or w>0.95 or tau_slow/tau_fast<2`. For a winning PL or SE, compare its fitted curve with the fitted E1 on integer t from the actual fit start through 120; if maximum absolute separation is `<0.05*sqrt(SSE_winner/n_fit)`, classify undetermined. An absent successful E1 fit means fit failure/undetermined. These are the inherited S01/S02a numerical definitions with the v2 start-specific domain made explicit. All four fits must have finite scores before any winner is declared. Score margin is >=2 against the runner-up; ties or any numerical failure are undetermined.

Calibration paths are never confirmatory recovery paths. No historical S02a pilot is rerun or included in a recovery denominator. After calibration, benchmark one complete point-fit panel without reporting classification; freeze retained processes, equal-N allocation, path IDs and seeds before any confirmatory fit. Uncalibratable processes get no simulated recovery rate. An empty confirmatory allocation gives blank rates, never 0%.
