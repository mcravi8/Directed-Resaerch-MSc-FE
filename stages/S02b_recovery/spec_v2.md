## Recovery design and classification
Only processes passing calibration enter recovery. Pre-freeze the calibrated process parameters, simulation cells, replication count N and keyed seeds before any recovery classification. Use the frozen detector, both anchors, common eligible events and 120-session S02a primary fit design on every retained path. Target execution well under 60 seconds per replication; benchmark the v2 pipeline before allocation, record actual full-path time, and choose a feasible equal N across all retained process × cell × anchor × start cells. If runtime binds, report completed N and planned N, with equal completed N across cells for confirmatory comparison; incomplete extras remain labeled exploratory/pending. Retain all planned path IDs and failed completed paths in denominators as undetermined. Do not resurrect S02a's N=min(100,floor(21600/(3*2*T_OU+4*2*T_2OU+2*2*T_ARFIMA))) formula: its N=0 is historical evidence, not v2 allocation.

Use the frozen point score n log(SSE/n)+2k at each start. A family wins only when its score is finite and beats every other fitted family by at least the frozen 2-point margin; otherwise classify undetermined. Preserve inherited E2 collapse and E1-like PL/SE degeneracy exclusions only after their numerical definition is resolved and frozen (see unresolved definitions). No per-replication bootstrap or bootstrap-based classification. Report true-process × winning-family × anchor × start confusion matrices with E1, E2, PL, SE and undetermined columns, planned/completed/failed/eligible denominators, undetermined share, nonconvergence and Wilson 95% intervals for each proportion. Report strict PL-only and broad PL/SE ARFIMA compatibility rates separately. A stochastic process's ACF family does not imply the exact conditional relaxation family.
